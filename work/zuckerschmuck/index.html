( The project )

Zuckerschmuck sells tapes and patches for CGM sensors, insulin-pump bags, sugar-free sweets and gifts for kids and adults with diabetes. The key UX problem is fit: a tape only helps if it matches your device.

So the homepage asks "Welches Gerät nutzt du?" (which device do you use?), with options from Dexcom G7 and FreeStyle Libre 3 to Omnipod and a dozen more, and sends shoppers straight to compatible products. The store runs in German, French, Spanish and English.
