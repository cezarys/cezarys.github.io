( The project )

The Ergotherapie-Verband Schweiz (EVS) represents occupational therapists across Switzerland. Its site serves members, the public and the press at the same time, in German, French and Italian.

Bold magenta and teal circles carry the brand, while the structure stays practical: news, the association, the profession, professional practice, education and research, plus job ads and a members-only area.
