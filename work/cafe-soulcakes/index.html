( The project )

Café Soulcakes bakes a 100% gluten-free range of cakes, cupcakes and cookies. The site does double duty: it is the café's home online, with the menu, opening hours, events and contact details, and a shop where customers order cakes and brunch boxes for home.

Soft pinks, serif headlines and floral cake photography give it a warm, celebratory feel.

What's on the site.

  • 01

    Café + shop in one

    Cakes, cupcakes, cookies, brunch boxes and B2B orders next to the café information.

  • 02

    Menu showcase

    A tabbed menu excerpt, from Shakshuka to French toast and açaí bowls, with photography for every dish.

  • 03

    Cake gallery

    Signature cakes like the Einhorn and Chocaholic Torte, each linking to the shop.

  • 04

    Clear expectations

    An opening-hours notice that makes it clear there are no reservations, so no surprises at the door.
