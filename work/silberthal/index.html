( The project )

Silberthal sells kitchen products to people who love cooking: stainless-steel pans, storage jars, and coffee and tea gear. The theme is product-first, with large lifestyle photography, tabbed bestsellers on the homepage and badges that make bundle savings obvious.

Trust signals sit wherever a shopper might hesitate: free shipping from €39, 100 days to try, free returns, and a Trusted Shops rating built on tens of thousands of reviews.

What's on the site.

  • 01

    Tabbed bestsellers

    Favourites, pans, storage jars, salad, coffee & tea and sets: one carousel with many entry points.

  • 02

    Clear savings

    Strike-through prices, "Spare X €" labels and percentage badges right on the product cards.

  • 03

    Editorial product blocks

    Full-width stories like "Deine Pfanne für scharfes Anbraten" that mix product and content.

  • 04

    Helpful empty cart

    An empty cart suggests bestsellers instead of being a dead end.

  • 05

    Mega menu

    Storage, coffee & tea, cookware, kitchen helpers, drinks and bundles, all one hover away.
