( The project )

PPC Designs runs Meta and Google Ads for startups and e-commerce brands. The site follows the agency's positioning: who they work with, how they work step by step, and what they deliver.

A vivid red-to-blue gradient hero and big, confident type set it apart from template agency sites, and a wall of client logos does the convincing.

What's on the site.

  • 01

    Who we work with

    Four audience cards, from go-to-market founders and stalled startups to scaling brands and international expansion, so visitors recognise themselves fast.

  • 02

    Service pages

    Campaign management, attribution & reporting, conversion tracking, CRO, ad creative and fractional CMO.

  • 03

    Process, step by step

    A numbered flow from building the tracking foundation to shaping the strategy.

  • 04

    Logos & blog

    A client logo wall, an insights blog and a founder page.
